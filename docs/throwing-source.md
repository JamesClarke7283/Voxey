# Throwable eggs and snowballs

Reference: installed Mineclonia source inspected 2026-09-16, with no Git metadata. Files: `mods/ITEMS/mcl_throwing/init.lua`, `mods/ITEMS/mcl_throwing/register.lua`, `mods/ENTITIES/mcl_mobs/breeding.lua`, `mods/ENTITIES/mcl_mobs/combat.lua`, `mods/ENTITIES/mobs_mc/blaze.lua`, `mods/CORE/mcl_util/misc.lua`, and `minetest.conf`. Reference license: [GPL-3.0](licenses/Mineclonia-GPL-3.0.txt).

Eggs and snowballs are usable projectiles, each stacking to sixteen. Right-click or the touch use button throws one item; survival consumes it and creative preserves it. Holding the control does not repeat throws, matching `place_was_held`. Existing creature and workstation interactions retain priority. The source sets no separate egg/snowball cooldown; Voxey retains its general 0.25-second use debounce.

The initial velocity is 22 blocks/second along the look direction, starting 1.5 blocks above the player's feet. Constant horizontal acceleration is −3 times the initial look direction. Gravity is 10.4 blocks/second², from this installed Mineclonia's configuration. The motion integration is independent of frame rate. Dispensers use the same velocity and decline to launch into a solid front block; droppers retain their ordinary item-ejection behavior.

Projectiles break against solid collision geometry and creatures, passing through water and other non-solid nodes. Substeps prevent tunneling through walls at low frame rates; walls are checked before actors. Voxey checks the actual creature body with a small projectile margin rather than the source's broad 1.5-block object-radius query. The thrower is ignored for the first half-second. A normal hit causes no health damage but retains provocation, fleeing and the source full-punch knockback formula. Snowballs deal three health damage to Blazes; eggs do not. Zero-damage hits avoid health-damage flashes and potion damage callbacks. Block hits activate existing target blocks with the source full-strength non-arrow pulse.

Only an egg's solid block impact can hatch chicks. It first rolls a 1-in-8 success; a successful hatch independently rolls a 1-in-32 chance of three additional chicks. This gives 224/256 no chicks, 31/256 one chick and 1/256 four chicks. Creature hits never hatch chicks. Hatching searches nearby space using the real baby collision box, adapting the source `spawn_child` / `prevent_phasing` behavior so babies are not embedded in walls. If no nearby loaded space fits, that chick is not spawned.

Hatched chicks start with the existing [farming lifecycle](farming-source.md): twenty-minute growth, baby dimensions, seed feeding, no egg laying before adulthood, and stable identities in the farming registry. Their ages and identities survive actual world saves, loading and distance-based hibernation without duplication. Hatching awards no breeding XP. Chickens supply eggs through their existing five-to-ten-minute laying timer. Shoveling naturally generated [top snow](snow-cover-source.md) supplies snowballs in survival; snow blocks and stacked layers have their source drop quantities.

The projectile mesh reuses Voxey's original pixel item artwork. Impact particles and sounds use the existing game effects. In-flight projectiles remain transient across world changes/reloads and expire after thirty seconds or on reaching unloaded space; those are explicit Voxey lifecycle limits, not a port of Mineclonia's entity static-data support. Existing ender-pearl and potion projectiles are separate and unchanged.

`tests/throwing_checks.gd` exercises live mouse/touch input, held-control suppression, station priority, survival/creative consumption, trajectory consistency, creature and wall collisions at multiple timesteps, Blaze damage, water traversal, target pulses, exact hatch probability branches, actual chick hatching, collision-safe baby size, dispensers, and real save/reload/hibernation restoration.
