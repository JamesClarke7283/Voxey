# Villages and the countryside

Use `/locate village` to find the nearest settlement, or explore the Overworld. Villages have thirteen homes, a well, paths, beds, lanterns, farms, job blocks and an iron golem. Villagers work during the day, go home at night, and seek shelter when the bell rings or monsters approach.

Right click a villager to trade. Every unlocked offer from the local Mineclonia reference is available: 297 offers across 13 professions and five levels. Stock, demand, two-input payments and XP are tracked separately. A trade only succeeds when payment is available and its output fits. Work at a reachable job block restores stock twice per day; the second restock needs more than two active minutes. Trading earns XP and improves that player’s reputation. Hitting villagers raises that player’s prices.

Ctrl + right click villagers with bread, carrots, potatoes or beetroots to feed them. Two fed adults and a spare bed allow a baby villager. Babies grow up over twenty active minutes. Unemployed adults can claim a nearby unoccupied workplace. Farmers harvest and replant nearby crops. Lead a villager to a new settlement, release it, and provide a reachable bed and workplace.

## Leads, animals and slimes

Craft two leads from five string and a slime ball using the recipe guide. Hold a lead and right click an animal or villager to attach it. Walk to pull it along; obstacles can block it, and excessive distance breaks the rope. Use a lead or an empty hand on the linked creature to release it and recover the lead. Linked creatures and ropes are saved, including when leaving a dimension.

Cows drop beef and leather. Sheep drop mutton and wool; shearing preserves the sheep. Chickens drop chicken and feathers. Rabbits drop meat, hides and sometimes a foot. Four rabbit hides make leather. Feed a horse three times, give it a saddle, then use it to ride; Ctrl dismounts. Leather horse armor reduces damage.

Slimes spawn on swamp surfaces on dark nights, weighted by the moon phase, and below Y −24 in seeded slime chunks regardless of light. About one in ten 16×16 chunks qualifies. Large slimes split into smaller ones, and the smallest drop slime balls. Swamps have muddy banks, grass, lily pads and kelp.

## Player homes

Choose or create a player profile on the title screen. `/sethome` saves that profile’s current location and facing in the current world. `/home` returns to it across any of the three dimensions. A player without a home receives instructions to set one. Profiles retain their identity when renamed. Homes are separate per player and per world; inventory and builds belong to the shared single-player world.

## Other workstations

Smokers cook food faster and blast furnaces smelt ore faster. Campfires cook without added fuel. Barrels store items. Composters turn seven plant items into bone meal. Fill cauldrons with a bucket to bottle water three times. Lecterns store signed books. Looms make colored banners; cartography tables make maps; stonecutters convert stone variants; smithing tables repair gear. Item frames preserve the displayed item’s metadata.

The fishing rod casts into water; reel in when bubbles appear. Boats launch on water and dismount with Ctrl. Read [alchemy and pouches](alchemy.md) for brewing, enchanted gear and portable storage.
