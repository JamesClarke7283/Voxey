# Mineclonia parity work

Status: **active, resumed on 2026-09-16 at the user’s request**. No claim of complete parity. The user-provided Mineclonia checkout is the reference, including its ore generation rules and world dimensions. The [next-feature audit](parity-next-audit.md) records inspected gaps and acceptance criteria.

The reproducible [source audit](mineclonia-audit.json) inventories 218 modules and 584 Lua files. Run `python3 tools/audit_mineclonia.py` to refresh it. Literal registration counts do not include all items generated by loops or helper functions.

## Acceptance rules

A module is complete only after its survival acquisition, behavior, interactions, persistence and applicable UI are verified against the reference. Matching names or adding creative-only items does not establish parity. Existing Voxey adaptations remain partial where their mechanics differ. Original user-requested features such as pouches, recovery chests and per-player homes remain supported.

User overrides from this session are deliberate: any wool color crafts into four string; four string in a 2×2 hand grid crafts a level-one pouch without a chest or wool. The conflicting string-to-wool recipe is removed. Swimming uses Space to rise and Shift to descend, including a collision-checked shore exit. See [swimming](swimming.md) and [pouch crafting](alchemy.md).

## Work queue

- Netherite: ancient debris, source ore placement, scrap/ingots/blocks, templates, smithing upgrades, equipment statistics and resistance.
- World generation: source bounds, vertical streaming, ore cluster/height/biome rules, terrain, caves, structures and all biomes.
- Combat and equipment: exact attack/armor rules, enchanting probabilities, anvil costs, shields, the [second hand](offhand-source.md), trims and effects.
- Blocks and mechanisms: missing building families, fluids, fire, plants, farming and remaining redstone edge cases. Rail transport and mineshaft generation are implemented.
- Creatures and encounters: remaining mobs, full AI, breeding/taming, Wither, raids, structure inhabitants and bosses.
- Player systems: hunger/saturation, achievements, maps, books, UI, weather and multiplayer/server behavior.

### Review corrections in this batch

- **Experience awards had to move, not just multiply.** Converting an award from
  `game.experience += n` to a thrown orb changes the *observable* contract: the
  balance no longer moves until the player walks over the orbs. Every existing
  check that pinned a direct credit was rewritten to assert the orb total and then
  the collection, never re-pinned to the new text (`alchemy_checks`,
  `fishing_checks`, `farming_checks`, `dungeon_checks`, `grindstone_repair_checks`).
- **The void's boundary was wrong in both directions.** The old check asserted
  damage five nodes below the floor; the source tolerates 64. The check now asserts
  the tolerance boundary *and* damages past it, and records that the shallow cells
  are bedrock, so suffocation applies there while the void does not.
- **Two bugs the new checks caught before they shipped.** `ItemPhysics.floats`
  sampled the cell at `+0.1` and so missed a resting item's own cell; it now reads
  the item's floor cell and its neighbour above. `push_direction` invented its own
  axis order; it is now the source's `cxcz` transcribed, including its
  positive-side-first rule for a coordinate below the cell centre.
- **Two real cross-test defects the batch exposed, both fixed.** The new
  offhand-first pickup left a torch in the second hand, which made Voxey's
  offhand-placement fallback fire on a *seed* in the main hand and swallow the
  planting click. The source reaches that helper from exactly two places — the
  empty hand's `on_place` and `mcl_tools.on_tool_place` — so it now fires only for
  an empty main hand or a tool, and it no longer places over a crop click.
  Separately, three existing check files mutated the player's world stance or the
  world node itself without restoring it (`offhand_checks` reloaded the world and
  left it processing, `firework_checks` flew the player to Y100 and left the
  elytra on, `pig_riding_checks` aimed from a camera transform an earlier group
  had left behind). Those are now captured and restored, and the pig fixture
  follows the suite's existing convention of setting the player's rotation, camera
  pitch and camera height before aiming.
- **A concurrent-suite artifact, not a regression.** Failures seen when several
  suites ran as parallel background jobs (`crop_farming` after `piston`, three
  `test_survival` checks) do not reproduce in foreground runs: the same pairs pass
  211/0 and 528/0. Suites must be run sequentially; the batch's numbers above are
  from sequential runs.

### Verification

All nine suites pass with no failures and no script errors: **7,947 checks** —
5,741 lifecycle, 856 village, 528 survival, 253 systems, 237 alchemy, 149
building, 71 parity, 64 polish, 48 environment. The batch's own groups are
`hazard` (54), `xp_orb` (54), `fish_bucket` (61), `bonus_chest` (23),
`grindstone_repair` (46), `witch_potion` (67), `sweet_berry` (32),
`cauldron_wash` (55), `spider_climb` (61), `redstone_ore` (32) and
`pig_riding` (57).

**Suites must be run one at a time.** Running several as parallel background
jobs starves the game loop, and the affected checks are input-timing sensitive:
the crop-planting and pig-mounting checks fail under contention and pass when the
same pairs run alone. The numbers above are from a sequential run of the nine
suites.

## Review corrections in the same batch

An independent review of the batch found defects that were then fixed and covered
by regression checks:

- **Concrete's sweep hardened one cell per second.** `harden` calls `set_node`,
  whose tail calls back into `Concrete.changed` and erases a key from the very
  dictionary the sweep was iterating; Godot ends a `Dictionary` loop on the first
  erase, so only one cell advanced per sweep. The sweep now iterates a key
  snapshot, which matches the source's `interval = 1, chance = 1` ABM.
- **Sculk, its vein and its catalyst dropped themselves.** The module's whole break
  contract (`harvest`, `drop_for`, `shears_drop`, `harvest_xp`) was unreferenced, so
  all three fell through to the generic drop. The break path now uses it: nothing
  by hand, the vein to shears, sculk and the catalyst to Silk Touch, and the stored
  experience on the break, as the source's `drop = ""` plus
  `_mcl_silk_touch_drop`/`_mcl_shears_drop` require.
- **A smithing template was never consumed.** Only the mineral was decremented, so
  one template trimmed unlimited armor. The source consumes all three inputs.
- **Soul fire emitted no light.** `NetherBlocks.light_level` existed but nothing
  consulted it; `Pasture.emission` now returns the source's `light_source = 10`.
- **The anvil's repair boosts used the wrong scale.** They were fractions of the
  source's normalised 0..65535 wear applied to Voxey's per-item durability, so a
  single plank fully repaired a wooden pickaxe and the four-material ladder was
  unreachable. The fraction is now taken of `Nodes.durability(id)`.
- **Armor repairs used the tool-tier ladder**, so iron armor asked for cobblestone
  and golden armor for iron. Armor now maps to its own material ladder, with
  chainmail on iron.
- **The prior-work penalty was dropped on load.** `clean_slot`'s allow-list did not
  carry `pwp`, so the anvil's escalating cost silently reset; it is now preserved.
- **Sprint knockback was a dead write.** `Creature` rebuilds its velocity from
  `knock` each step, so the direct write was overwritten before use; the impulse
  now goes through `knock`.
- **Splash and lingering potions never dispensed.** `Dispensers.is_potion` read
  `splash`/`lingering` keys that do not exist in `VillageContent.DATA`; the
  throwable form lives in `PotionCatalog.ITEMS[id].form`, which is now what it
  reads. A dispensed XP bottle also now grants its experience where it lands.
- **Six sound kinds had no duration entry**, so their samples were never built and
  every play was a silent no-op; they now have both a duration and a match branch.
- **Building-shape ids collided.** Appending materials to `BuildingShapes.MATERIALS`
  grew its computed range into the barrier families at 5000, which put the wood
  species' slabs inside the appended band. The added materials now live in their own
  verified-free band, and a check asserts the original ids are unchanged.
- **Achievements were partly inert.** The set was extended with the source's
  remaining reachable titles; every one now has a call site, and five that Voxey's
  systems genuinely cannot support (no axolotl, no cat/parrot/dolphin lead target,
  no lightning-on-a-villager path, no rail odometer, no fish-bucketing action) were
  removed rather than listed as unreachable.
