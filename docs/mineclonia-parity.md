# Mineclonia parity work

Status: **paused at the user’s request after the environment fixes**. No claim of complete parity. The user-provided Mineclonia checkout is the reference, including its ore generation rules and world dimensions.

The reproducible [source audit](mineclonia-audit.json) inventories 218 modules and 584 Lua files. Run `python3 tools/audit_mineclonia.py` to refresh it. Literal registration counts do not include all items generated by loops or helper functions.

## Acceptance rules

A module is complete only after its survival acquisition, behavior, interactions, persistence and applicable UI are verified against the reference. Matching names or adding creative-only items does not establish parity. Existing Voxey adaptations remain partial where their mechanics differ. Original user-requested features such as pouches, recovery chests and per-player homes remain supported.

## Work queue

- Netherite: ancient debris, source ore placement, scrap/ingots/blocks, templates, smithing upgrades, equipment statistics and resistance.
- World generation: source bounds, vertical streaming, ore cluster/height/biome rules, terrain, caves, structures and all biomes.
- Combat and equipment: exact attack/armor rules, enchanting probabilities, anvil costs, shields, offhand, trims and effects.
- Blocks and mechanisms: missing building families, fluids, fire, plants, farming, redstone edge cases, minecarts and transport.
- Creatures and encounters: remaining mobs, full AI, breeding/taming, Wither, raids, structure inhabitants and bosses.
- Player systems: hunger/saturation, achievements, maps, books, UI, weather and multiplayer/server behavior.

## Current implementation evidence

- Sparse source world bounds, 100 exported legacy mineral scatter rules and an updated deepslate transition are implemented. Source terrain, missing biomes, blobs and optional generator presets remain open.
- Netherite survival upgrades, armor toughness/wear, lava immunity and old-save migration are implemented and tested.
- Bastion loot, persistent piglin/brute residents, all 18 barter entries, fire ignition/spread and lodestone compasses have automated checks. Full structure layouts, detailed AI, several loot-consuming systems and complete fire behavior remain open.
- Offline identity is fixed to `player`; the selector is removed. Homes, reputation and recovery ownership retain player keys and migrate from the previously active identity.

- The user-requested [performance and interface fixes](performance-and-ui.md) are implemented with CPU benchmarks, rendered GPU checks and inventory/deletion regression coverage. These do not establish full source parity.
- The first [51 stair and slab families](building-shapes.md) support placement, corners, collision, crafting and saves. All 5,000 reference corner cases agree. Eight deepslate/tuff masonry blocks and natural source-parameter tuff blobs are included; remaining material families and other building shapes are still open.

- The [eating, daylight and fluid-flow update](environment-update.md) adds hold-to-eat timing, daylight checks near cover and persistent downward water/lava flow. Broader content work is paused.
