# Mineclonia parity work

Status: **active, resumed on 2026-09-16 at the user’s request**. No claim of complete parity. The user-provided Mineclonia checkout is the reference, including its ore generation rules and world dimensions. The [next-feature audit](parity-next-audit.md) records inspected gaps and acceptance criteria.

The reproducible [source audit](mineclonia-audit.json) inventories 218 modules and 584 Lua files. Run `python3 tools/audit_mineclonia.py` to refresh it. Literal registration counts do not include all items generated by loops or helper functions.

## Acceptance rules

A module is complete only after its survival acquisition, behavior, interactions, persistence and applicable UI are verified against the reference. Matching names or adding creative-only items does not establish parity. Existing Voxey adaptations remain partial where their mechanics differ. Original user-requested features such as pouches, recovery chests and per-player homes remain supported.

User overrides from this session are deliberate: any wool color crafts into four string; four string in a 2×2 hand grid crafts a level-one pouch without a chest or wool. The conflicting string-to-wool recipe is removed. Swimming uses Space to rise and Shift to descend, including a collision-checked shore exit. See [swimming](swimming.md) and [pouch crafting](alchemy.md).

## Work queue

- Netherite: ancient debris, source ore placement, scrap/ingots/blocks, templates, smithing upgrades, equipment statistics and resistance.
- World generation: source bounds, vertical streaming, ore cluster/height/biome rules, terrain, caves, structures and all biomes.
- Combat and equipment: exact attack/armor rules, enchanting probabilities, anvil costs, shields, the [second hand](offhand-source.md), trims and effects.
- Blocks and mechanisms: missing building families, fluids, fire, plants, farming and remaining redstone edge cases. Rail transport and mineshaft generation are implemented.
- Creatures and encounters: remaining mobs, full AI, breeding/taming, Wither, raids, structure inhabitants and bosses.
- Player systems: hunger/saturation, achievements, maps, books, UI, weather and multiplayer/server behavior.

## Review corrections in the same batch

An independent review of the batch found defects that were then fixed and covered
by regression checks:

- **Concrete's sweep hardened one cell per second.** `harden` calls `set_node`,
  whose tail calls back into `Concrete.changed` and erases a key from the very
  dictionary the sweep was iterating; Godot ends a `Dictionary` loop on the first
  erase, so only one cell advanced per sweep. The sweep now iterates a key
  snapshot, which matches the source's `interval = 1, chance = 1` ABM.
- **Sculk, its vein and its catalyst dropped themselves.** The module's whole break
  contract (`harvest`, `drop_for`, `shears_drop`, `harvest_xp`) was unreferenced, so
  all three fell through to the generic drop. The break path now uses it: nothing
  by hand, the vein to shears, sculk and the catalyst to Silk Touch, and the stored
  experience on the break, as the source's `drop = ""` plus
  `_mcl_silk_touch_drop`/`_mcl_shears_drop` require.
- **A smithing template was never consumed.** Only the mineral was decremented, so
  one template trimmed unlimited armor. The source consumes all three inputs.
- **Soul fire emitted no light.** `NetherBlocks.light_level` existed but nothing
  consulted it; `Pasture.emission` now returns the source's `light_source = 10`.
- **The anvil's repair boosts used the wrong scale.** They were fractions of the
  source's normalised 0..65535 wear applied to Voxey's per-item durability, so a
  single plank fully repaired a wooden pickaxe and the four-material ladder was
  unreachable. The fraction is now taken of `Nodes.durability(id)`.
- **Armor repairs used the tool-tier ladder**, so iron armor asked for cobblestone
  and golden armor for iron. Armor now maps to its own material ladder, with
  chainmail on iron.
- **The prior-work penalty was dropped on load.** `clean_slot`'s allow-list did not
  carry `pwp`, so the anvil's escalating cost silently reset; it is now preserved.
- **Sprint knockback was a dead write.** `Creature` rebuilds its velocity from
  `knock` each step, so the direct write was overwritten before use; the impulse
  now goes through `knock`.
- **Splash and lingering potions never dispensed.** `Dispensers.is_potion` read
  `splash`/`lingering` keys that do not exist in `VillageContent.DATA`; the
  throwable form lives in `PotionCatalog.ITEMS[id].form`, which is now what it
  reads. A dispensed XP bottle also now grants its experience where it lands.
- **Six sound kinds had no duration entry**, so their samples were never built and
  every play was a silent no-op; they now have both a duration and a match branch.
- **Building-shape ids collided.** Appending materials to `BuildingShapes.MATERIALS`
  grew its computed range into the barrier families at 5000, which put the wood
  species' slabs inside the appended band. The added materials now live in their own
  verified-free band, and a check asserts the original ids are unchanged.
- **Achievements were partly inert.** The set was extended with the source's
  remaining reachable titles; every one now has a call site, and five that Voxey's
  systems genuinely cannot support (no axolotl, no cat/parrot/dolphin lead target,
  no lightning-on-a-villager path, no rail odometer, no fish-bucketing action) were
  removed rather than listed as unreachable.

### Later in the same batch

- **Zombie sieges** ([`zombie_siege.gd`](../scripts/zombie_siege.gd)): the source's
  midnight one-in-ten roll at a village, its 20 x 10 summon attempts at offsets of up
  to eight blocks, and the event ending when the summoned zombies are gone. The
  source's `get_poi_heat` score has no analogue in Voxey, so the heat test is the
  count of living village residents near the point, which is what the score is for.
  The checkout's mushroom-biome guard has no analogue either and is recorded rather
  than invented.
- **The wandering trader and its llamas** ([`wandering_traders.gd`](../scripts/wandering_traders.gd)):
  the source's nine-offer pool drawn as two purchasing, two special and five ordinary
  offers priced in emeralds, the twenty-minute life before it walks away, the two-llama
  escort sharing that timer, the day/night invisibility, and the spawner whose delay
  only reaches its limit after twenty sixty-second ticks with the chance rising 25 to
  75. The trader's record is served through the same `VillageLife.record` lookup a
  villager uses, so the existing trading panel displays and performs its trades
  unchanged. The escort is hosted on `RuralAnimal`, which is where Voxey keeps its
  quadrupeds. The source's llama taming, riding and spit are omitted: the trader llama
  is explicitly untameable in the reference, and Voxey has neither a wolf to spit at
  nor a llama-spit projectile.
- **The remaining raid roles** ([`raid_mobs.gd`](../scripts/raid_mobs.gd)): the
  **ravager** (100 health, 12 damage, the source's 0.75 knockback resistance and 1.5

  (14 health, 4 damage, passes through blocks, takes no fall or environmental damage,
  and lives on a `30 + random(90)` second clock after which it takes constant damage
  rather than despawning). Both have their own models. The raid itself now follows
  the source's `mobs_and_spawn_count_by_wave` table instead of a fixed pillager
  count, so later waves bring vindicators, evokers, witches and ravagers, and the
  ordinary wave count is the source's `1 + difficulty * 2`.
- **The crimson and warped nether plants** ([`crimson_plants.gd`](../scripts/crimson_plants.gd)):
  both fungi with the source's `light_source = 1` and its `soil_fungus` binding, the
  crimson and warped roots, nether sprouts and the two climbable vines — all of which
  drop only to shears — plus the warped wart block. Bone meal grows a fungus into its
  huge form on the source's 40% roll: a stem of three to five blocks carrying a
  wart-block cap with shroomlight set into it, which is the structure the source's own
  `mcl_crimson/schematics/*.mts` encode. Voxey has no `.mts` reader, so the shape is
  generated rather than loaded, and a blocked column refuses the growth outright.
- **The pale oak family** ([`pale_oak.gd`](../scripts/pale_oak.gd)): the resin chain
  (clump → block of resin → smelted resin brick → resin brick block, with a chiseled
  variant and the block's own nine-clump decraft), and resin is a trim material, as
  the source's `_mcl_armor_trim_color` on the resin brick says. Plus the pale hanging
  moss that grows downward to the source's maximum length of eight with a distinct tip
  state, the pale moss block and its carpet with the source's `random(2,3)`-square
  bone-meal spread, and the eyeblossom that opens at night on the source's two-second
  ABM. The tree's own wooden family belongs to `WoodTypes` and needs the PaleGarden
  biome, so it is out of scope. `resin_clump` has **no acquisition route** in the
  checkout — the creaking that drops it is not in this reference — and is recorded
  rather than given a recipe that would not be the source's.
- **The copper decorative families** ([`copper_decor.gd`](../scripts/copper_decor.gd)):
  the copper **lantern** in its floor and ceiling placements, the copper **chain** and
  the copper **bars**, each in all four oxidation stages. They are ordinary members of
  the existing `Copper` engine — their stage arrays are appended to `Copper.CHAINS`, so
  they oxidise on the same 500-second sweep, freeze when waxed with honeycomb, lose
  their wax or a stage to an axe, and de-oxidise when struck by lightning, through
  exactly the code the copper blocks already use. A ceiling-aimed lantern places the
  ceiling form. The source's copper **torch** is absent from this checkout
  (`mcl_torches` registers no copper variant), so it is not invented.
- **The chiseled bookshelf** ([`bookshelves.gd`](../scripts/bookshelves.gd)): six slots of
  exactly one book each, a right-click that puts a book into or takes one out of the
  slot under the pointer, a hopper above that feeds one book per transfer, the books
  returned on breaking, and — the part that makes it a redstone component — a
  comparator reading `last_slot_used`, so a shelf reports the slot that last changed
  rather than how full it is. The source gets its per-slot display by registering 64
  nodes; Voxey keeps one id and draws the filled slots from saved state, which is the
  same pattern it already uses for candles and shulker contents.
- **All twenty-three pottery sherds** ([`archaeology.gd`](../scripts/archaeology.gd)): the
  four registered patterns are now the full source set, each with its own procedural
  motif. Every one that has a reachable item route is filled into the ocean-ruins and
  desert-temple loot tables by pattern name, so the new sherds are survival-obtainable
  from the same archaeology the first four were. Every non-sherd entry in those tables
  now names a real Voxey item too, including the source's wooden hoe and iron axe, so
  no table entry is a zero-id placeholder.
- **Hopper and minecart exchange**: a hopper pulls from a chest or hopper minecart one
  block above and pushes into one a block below, within the source's 1.5-block reach,
  one item per transfer.
- **The anvil's damage ladder** now exists on the block: the two damaged states are
  registered, every use rolls the source's twelve percent, and the third level
  destroys the anvil and returns it as a drop.
