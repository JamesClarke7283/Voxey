# Adhesive piston movement

Reference: installed Mineclonia `mods/ITEMS/REDSTONE/mcl_pistons/api.lua` and `nodes.lua`, plus slime/honey and each moved block's group definitions. `scripts/piston_push.gd` replaces Voxey's linear-only push with the source breadth-first movement frontier.

Attribution: Mineclonia and MineClone contributors. Adapted source algorithms follow [GPL-3.0-or-later](licenses/Mineclonia-GPL-3.0.txt); Voxey meshes, textures and UI artwork are original.

A piston follows forward obstructions and adhesive side branches. Honey and slime attach to ordinary movable neighbors on all six sides but do not attach to each other; either can still push the other through a direct forward obstruction. Immovable side neighbors are ignored. An immovable forward obstruction anywhere in the assembly aborts the entire move. The piston itself cannot become part of its own moved assembly. Up to twelve ordinary nodes move; source fragile nodes are destroyed without consuming that limit. Sticky retraction uses the same planner in the opposite direction and can pull an adhesive assembly, while unsticky blocks remain behind.

Explicit fragile handling preserves existing buttons/plates, doors, top snow and cake, and adds ordinary/wall, soul and redstone torches, item frames, raw pumpkin, melon, carved pumpkin, jack o'lantern and amethyst crystal/budding states. Each destruction callback runs once; paired doors cannot duplicate drops. Frames drop their stored item with its exact metadata; banners retain the source immovable flag. Source crop stems have neither the explicit dig-by-piston nor unsticky group: they move with saved growth metadata, then validate their final support. Environmental fruit/amethyst drops ignore a player's creative mode and held enchantments. This table is limited to the implemented block families; it does not claim a complete translation of every Mineclonia node group.

The planner captures node metadata and all movable station data, including lectern books, sign text/appearance and hive production timers, before writing any blocks. Old station keys are removed rather than leaving duplicate records behind. All moved nodes share one translation, so farthest-first writes preserve overlapping branches. Attachment checks are deferred until the final arrangement and piston head exist. Stems, crystals, snow, cake, flowers, signs, torches and redstone attachments then retain surviving support or break exactly once when support is actually absent.

Players, mobs, mounts, boats, dropped items, primed TNT, falling nodes and implemented projectile entities participate in movement. Boat passengers and mounted riders are validated with their carrier, including their full headroom. Player/mob/boat shifts are checked against the predicted final block geometry and swept stationary obstacles before the push commits. Fence/wall/stair geometry is recomputed from future neighbors, including stationary neighbors whose connections change. This preserves Voxey's existing collision-prevention behavior: a trapped actor can prevent the push rather than being embedded in blocks. Blocked loose entities follow the source's skip behavior and cannot jam a machine. Drops created by destruction are transported after the move too, so downstream hoppers can collect them; a bounded face escape handles a blocked forward path without crossing surrounding walls. Fully enclosed drops are not teleported through solid blocks. Actors above a moving block are carried, and slime contact applies the source category-dependent launch velocity. Honey carries without slime launch. Source point/radius object detection is adapted to Voxey's body bounds; mount/boat passengers follow their carrier once.

Existing Voxey container/portal/spawner immovability remains in `RedstoneCircuit.movable`; this batch does not implement Mineclonia's optional movable-inventory setting. Source short-pulse sticky-piston detachment and delayed extension timing also remain outside this planner.

Tests: `tests/piston_checks.gd` exercises actual extension/retraction, twelve/thirteen-block boundaries, source fragile drops, movable stems, sign metadata, surviving/removed attachment support, actor rollback, carrying, slime launch, TNT/projectile origins and loose-item behavior. `tests/beehive_checks.gd` adds hive timer/facing and adhesive assembly checks; `tests/amethyst_checks.gd` covers crystal destruction, unsticky retraction and support replacement.
